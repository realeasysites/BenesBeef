Bene's = Benedetto = "blessed"

A Chef-Driven Beef Shop. Inside a Gas Station.

Bene's is Chef TJ Castiello's family shop. TJ spent about 20 years cooking in kitchens across Boston, Portland and Lewiston, including a stint at the original Kelly's Roast Beef on Revere Beach, home of the North Shore beef. He brought that sandwich to Lisbon Street and built a whole menu of big, loud, homemade food around it.

The name comes from the family name, Benedetto, Italian for "blessed." TJ's dad Tommy helps run the shop. There's no freezer in the kitchen. The beef is cooked fresh every day, the fries are hand-cut and double-fried in beef tallow, and the potatoes come from right up the road at Bell Farms in Auburn.

"I just want to make people happy."

— Chef TJ Castiello
